
# 🌟 Performance Review Preparation – Self-Assessment Summary Generator 💼

![Sample](https://github.com/ondrejvysek/copilot-prompts/blob/main/samples/m365-performance-review-prep/assets/sample.png)

## Summary

This prompt is designed for executives and team leads to prepare for performance reviews or feedback sessions by generating a **concise, professional self-assessment summary**. It aggregates insights from your enterprise communications—emails, chats, meetings, and shared documents—and organizes them into clear sections:

- Role & Scope
- Key Contributions
- Strengths
- Development Opportunities
- Forward-Looking Focus

The output is ideal for formal reviews, 1:1 discussions, or submission in HR systems. It ensures alignment with organizational goals and leadership expectations.

---

## Prompt 💡
```
Help me prepare for a performance review or feedback session with my manager. Please include all my communications—emails, chats, meetings, and shared documents—and provide a summary of my contributions, strengths, and development opportunities.
```

## Description ℹ️

This prompt provides a structured method to prepare for a performance review or feedback session by generating a **concise self-assessment summary**. It aggregates insights from your enterprise communications—emails, chats, meetings, and shared documents—and organizes them into clear sections: Role & Scope, Key Contributions, Strengths, Development Opportunities, and Forward-Looking Focus. The output ensures alignment with organizational goals and leadership expectations, saving time and improving clarity.

## Instructions 📝

1. Make sure you have Copilot for Microsoft 365 in your tenant (Work tab available).
2. Go to Microsoft 365 Copilot in Office.com or use Copilot chat in Teams.
3. Switch to GPT-5 which is optional for this prompt.
4. Copy & paste the above prompt.
5. Adjust language or add specific metrics as needed.
6. Copilot will generate a structured self-assessment summary based on your enterprise data.
7. Use the output for your performance review or feedback session.

### Improvise Usage 🚀
- Convert the summary into a polished Word document for HR submission.
- Create a slide deck for your 1:1 discussion.
- Automate quarterly self-assessment generation with Power Automate.
- Use the output as a baseline for goal-setting in the next review cycle.
- Share the summary with your manager for alignment before the formal review.
